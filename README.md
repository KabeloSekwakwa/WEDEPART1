# WEDEPART1
Project Name: Kay's Fragrance
Kabelo Sekwakwa ST10509287
History: Founded in 2022 by a passionate fragrance enthusiast, Kay’s Fragrance began as a local boutique pop-up aimed at bridging the gap between high-end luxury scents and affordability. The business quickly gained a loyal community following through word-of-mouth and social media by creating long-lasting, premium-inspired perfumes and colognes without the designer markup.
Mission Statement: To empower every individual to express their unique identity through high-quality, long-lasting fragrances made accessible and affordable to all.
Vision Statement: To become the leading online destination for luxury-quality, budget-friendly perfumes and colognes across South Africa.

Specific Goals:
•	Launch a modern, responsive e-commerce web platform to expand reach beyond local pop-ups.
•	Drive direct online sales of perfumes and colognes.
•	Build brand loyalty and establish Kay's Fragrance as a trusted online scent retailer.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
•	Conversion Rate: Achieve an average online sales conversion rate of 2.5% to 3.5% within the first six months.
•	Traffic Growth: Target 5,000+ monthly unique visitors within 4 months of launch.
•	Average Order Value (AOV): Maintain an AOV of R450 through product bundling and upsell options.
•	Cart Abandonment Rate: Keep cart abandonment below 60% through a streamlined checkout process.

Essential Pages:
Homepage: Dynamic hero banner showcasing featured scents, best-sellers, seasonal promotions, and clear Call-to-Actions (CTAs).
 Shop / Catalog Page: Filterable gallery by fragrance family (e.g., Floral, Woody, Fresh, Oriental), gender (Men, Women, Unisex), and price.
About Us Page: The story of Kay’s Fragrance, brand values, and commitment to affordability.
Contact & FAQ Page: Contact form, operating hours, shipping policies, return policy, and answers to common scent queries.
Desired Functionality:
•	Integrated Secure Checkout (Payment gateways like PayFast/SnapScan/Ozow).
•	Customer Review & Rating System.
•	Search bar with real-time auto-suggestions.

Milestone 1: Requirement gathering, research, and proposal finalisation (Week 1–2)
Milestone 2: Low & High-Fidelity Wireframes, UI/UX Design Approval (Week 3–4)
Milestone 3: Development (HTML, CSS, JS) (Week 5–7)
Milestone 4: Product integration, Payment Gateway & Shipping Setup (Week 8)
Milestone 5: Testing (Cross-browser, mobile responsiveness, payment testing) (Week 9)
Milestone 6: Deployment, Final Submission, and Official Launch (Week 10)

Hosting & Domain Requirements:
•	Domain: www.kaysfragrance.co.za 
•	Hosting: High-speed cloud web hosting with 99.9% uptime, SSL Certificate (HTTPS) for secure transactions, and daily backups.
Programming Languages & Frameworks:
•	Front-End: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
•	Styling/Frameworks: Bootstrap 5 or Tailwind CSS for fast responsive styling.
•	Back-End / CMS: WordPress with WooCommerce or custom Node.js/PHP backend depending on scale requirements.
