About Kay's Fragrance

Our History

Founded in 2022 by a passionate fragrance enthusiast, Kay's Fragrance began as a local boutique pop-up aimed at bridging the gap between high-end luxury scents and affordability. The business quickly gained a loyal community following through word-of-mouth and social media by creating long-lasting, premium-inspired perfumes and colognes without the designer markup.

Our Mission

To empower every individual to express their unique identity through high-quality, long-lasting fragrances made accessible and affordable to all.

Our Vision

To become the leading online destination for luxury-quality, budget-friendly perfumes and colognes across South Africa.
